# Artwork and attribution

The visual direction draws on early terminal computing, while original project
art, third-party references, and preserved historical material keep their own
provenance. A reference's presence in a research note does not establish a right
to redistribute it.

David Schmenk's [Apple1-Slideshow](../docs/reference/apple1-slideshow.md) is a
pinned heritage reference. Its redistribution license has not been established;
its artwork is not vendored here. Keep original project material and historical
references distinguishable in presentations and exports.

The software also builds on third-party components, including
[Py65](https://github.com/mnaberez/py65). Use the source and provenance records
when describing what the project implements and what it reuses.
