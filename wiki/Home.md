# Apple-1 project wiki

[Worked textbook: 15 chapters, 110 labs, BASIC and the card edition](Textbook.md)

Welcome. This project combines a Replica 1 Plus collection, Apple-1 programming
lessons, and NEURAL1: a software lab that asks small language models to work
within early-computer constraints.

Use this wiki as a guided introduction. Detailed procedures and primary records
live in `docs/`; the [project map](../docs/PROJECT-MAP.md) is the full directory.
These pages are versioned in the repository's `wiki/` folder and can be read
directly on GitHub or in a checkout.

## Work through the textbook

The [worked textbook](../docs/textbook/README.md) provides 15 teaching chapters,
companions for all 42 lessons, eight local-model labs, and 36 feature examples.
Its 110 runnable examples include captured output and images, deeper CPU
tutorials, six real BASIC programs, four card labs, and fresh local-model/Pi records. Use the
[course guide](../docs/textbook/course-guide.md) or
[native walkthrough](../docs/textbook/native/README.md). The
[coverage register](../docs/textbook/coverage.md) distinguishes actual host/virtual
execution, fixtures, retained native records, and operator-only procedures.

## Know which part you are looking at

**COMPUTER** means ordinary Apple-1 computing. **Field Library** means lessons
and source-grounded explanations. **NEURAL1** means virtual experiments.
**META/1** is the claims-and-evidence system supporting those experiments.

The software runs on modern hardware; the Apple-1 execution world is virtual.
The September Pi acceptance record and the unresolved physical Replica
integration are different workstreams. Read [current status](../docs/STATUS.md)
for their scope and remaining work.
