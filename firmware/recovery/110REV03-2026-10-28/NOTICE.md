# Attribution and source notices

The firmware is the preserved Briel Computers / ReActiveMicro `replica 110REV03`
source, with its included serial, keyboard, font and TV objects. The original
files, authorship/comments and embedded notices remain under
`firmware/vendor/110REV03/` in both repository and downloadable bundle.

- Source/provenance: https://wiki.reactivemicro.com/Replica_1 and the preserved
  `firmware/vendor/110REV03/provenance.json`.
- OpenSpin: https://github.com/parallaxinc/OpenSpin at
  `d1991aa821179209cbd21396dfd1d88f226eb99d`. Parallax compiler sources carry
  their MIT permission notices, included in the complete source snapshot.
- PropLoader: https://github.com/parallaxinc/PropLoader at
  `a1b4cd87cedfb1141d9be888335bca130b486425`. Its source snapshot includes
  `LICENSE`, `license.txt` and file-level copyright/permission notices.

The source archives retain those full notices alongside the supplied tool
executables. The firmware components have their own original attribution and
notices; this package makes no blanket claim that all third-party material is
covered by a repository-wide license. No authorship of the vendor firmware is
claimed. Project contributions here are reproducible staging, validation,
packaging, operational instructions and the recovery evidence record.
