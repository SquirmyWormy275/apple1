# Source register: Apple-1 displays and Sanyo monitors

**Last reviewed:** 2026-08-30

This register distinguishes contemporary/primary technical sources,
institutional object records, later provenance material, and research leads.
Links are external references; inclusion here does not imply permission to copy
images or complete documents. Media rights are reviewed separately in
[image-rights-ledger.md](image-rights-ledger.md).

## Apple / Apple-1 primary and institutional sources

### A1 — Apple-1 Operation Manual, 1976

- **Type:** contemporary Apple technical documentation
- **Host:** Computer History Museum archive
- **URL:** https://archive.computerhistory.org/resources/text/Apple/Apple.AppleI.1976.102646518.pdf
- **Supports:** composite-positive video; standard raster monitor; TV through RF
  modulator; Motorola and Ball named as example monitor manufacturers; basic
  display characteristics.
- **Does not support:** a dedicated Apple-1 monitor model.
- **Reuse:** link and short quotation/paraphrase only; no redistribution license
  for the complete scan was established in this review.

### A2 — Apple-1 Registry, Peripherals

- **Type:** specialist secondary reference / registry
- **URL:** https://www.apple1registry.com/en/peripherals.html
- **Supports:** Apple did not offer a particular Apple-1 monitor; many TVs and
  monitors were used; VM-4209/VM-4509 became prominent in famous auction images.
- **Caution:** later secondary source. Use for collector-history interpretation,
  not as a substitute for 1976 Apple documentation.
- **Reuse:** link/paraphrase. Do not copy Registry imagery without separate
  permission/license review.

### A3 — Sotheby's 2026 original-owner Apple-1 listing

- **Type:** auction provenance / secondary artifact record
- **URL:** https://www.sothebys.com/en/buy/auction/2026/history-of-science-technology-2/apple-1-computer?locale=en
- **Supports:** surviving original-owner Apple-1 ensemble includes a vintage
  Hitachi VM-906AU monitor.
- **Does not support:** same-day purchase of that monitor without a transaction
  document.
- **Reuse:** link/paraphrase only; photographs not licensed here.

### A4 — The Henry Ford, Apple 1 Computer, 1976

- **Type:** museum object record
- **URL:** https://www.thehenryford.org/collections/explore/artifact/400080
- **Supports:** ensemble monitor label transcribed as Sanyo VM4209, 120 V 60 Hz
  32 W, manufactured June 1979.
- **Does not support:** original 1976 monitor pairing.
- **Reuse:** object metadata can be cited; image reuse not assumed.

### A5 — Christie's, “The first Apple computer” collection

- **Type:** auction/collection catalog
- **URL:** https://onlineonly.christies.com/s/shoulders-giants-making-modern-world/first-apple-computer-50/70080
- **Supports:** curated collection includes working VM4209 tested with Apple-1.
- **Does not support:** original 1976 acquisition with that Apple-1.
- **Reuse:** link/paraphrase only; photographs not licensed here.

### A6 — Retrocomputing Stack Exchange monitor discussion

- **Type:** community secondary discussion
- **URL:** https://retrocomputing.stackexchange.com/questions/11281/did-apple-bundle-a-specific-monitor-with-the-apple-ii-for-schools
- **Supports:** documents a widely repeated claim that a 1977 Apple II ad shows a
  VM-4209.
- **Caution:** this research did not recover an authenticated primary ad from the
  cited chain. The claim therefore remains `UNVERIFIED`.

### A7 — Apple II advertisement, December 1977 BYTE

- **Type:** contemporary Apple advertisement; public-domain scan in U.S.
- **Page 1:** https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Apple_II_advertisement_Dec_1977_page_1.jpg
- **Page 2:** https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Apple_II_advertisement_Dec_1977_page_2.jpg
- **Supports:** Apple marketed the Apple II with a color television and explicitly
  described plugging it into a standard color TV.
- **Rights:** Commons records the advertisement as public domain in the United
  States because it was published without a separate copyright notice. Other
  jurisdictions may differ. See image-rights ledger.

### A8 — V&A digital-design collecting interview, 2020

- **Type:** institutional secondary statement
- **URL:** https://ejournals.eu/pliki_artykulu_czasopisma/pelny_tekst/cc7260c2-efca-4fa9-bc57-70bcffe247e5/pobierz
- **Supports:** V&A curator says Apple II was collected alongside the same Sanyo
  monitor model Jobs used to take to trade fairs.
- **Does not support:** event/date or underlying identification method; request
  curatorial evidence before upgrading this claim.

## Sanyo sources

### S1 — Jimmy Grewal collection inventory

- **Type:** private collector specimen inventory
- **URL:** https://www.jimmygrewal.com/collection/
- **Supports:** VM-4209 specimens listed as February 1977 and November 1977.
- **Caution:** not a Sanyo factory production record.

### S2 — Sanyo VM-4209 service manual, Internet Archive

- **Type:** scan of manufacturer service literature
- **URL:** https://archive.org/details/manual_VM4209_SM_SANYO_EN
- **OCR text:** https://archive.org/stream/manual_VM4209_SM_SANYO_EN/VM4209_SM_SANYO_EN_djvu.txt
- **Supports:** technical data for the archived 625-line / 220 V / 50 Hz variant,
  including switchable high/75-ohm input and >600-line horizontal resolution.
- **Caution:** do not generalize those regional electrical/video specifications
  to U.S. units.
- **Reuse:** no republication license established; link only.

### S3 — Smithsonian Sanyo VM-4092 object

- **Type:** museum physical-object record
- **URL:** https://americanhistory.si.edu/collections/object/nmah_1318801
- **Supports:** label dates monitor February 1976; donor used it with IMSAI 8080.
- **Rights:** object page states usage conditions apply; image not copied.

### S4 — Smithsonian VM-4092 service-manual collection

- **Type:** museum documentation record
- **URL:** https://americanhistory.si.edu/collections/object/nmah_1371308
- **Supports:** archive includes Sanyo VM4092 service manual, including a 1973
  manual and a revised manual.
- **Rights:** link to catalog metadata; do not assume scan/image reuse rights.

### S5 — Applefritter, VM-4092 vs VM-4209 discussion

- **Type:** collector/restorer testimony and physical observations
- **URL:** https://www.applefritter.com/content/differences-between-sanyo-vm-4209-and-vm-4092-monitor
- **Supports:** June 1976 VM-4092 in original-owner Apple-1 chain; 1977 VM-4209
  with original-owner Sol-20; observed model differences and date overlap.
- **Caution:** not factory literature or transaction-level documentation.

### S6 — Ohio Scientific Challenger advertisement, BYTE May 1977

- **Type:** contemporary computer advertisement
- **URL:** https://www.worldradiohistory.com/Archive-Byte/70s/Byte-1977-05.pdf
- **Supports:** complete Challenger system offered with stand-alone terminal and
  **Sanyo monitor** for 16 lines x 64 characters at 2400 baud.
- **Does not support:** monitor model number.

### S7 — Micro Systems Development advertisement, BYTE January 1978

- **Type:** contemporary computer advertisement
- **URL:** https://vintageapple.org/byte/pdf/197801_Byte_Magazine_Vol_03-01_The_Brains_of_Men_and_Machines.pdf
- **Supports:** explicit `Sanyo Monitor (VM4209) $150` listing.

### S8 — April 1978 Apple-property VM-4209, RR Auction

- **Does not support:** Apple acquisition date, department, or Apple-1 use.
- **Rights:** auction photos not copied; no reuse grant found.

### S9 — Computer Warehouse advertisement, BYTE May 1978

- **URL:** https://vintageapple.org/byte/pdf/197805_Byte_Magazine_Vol_03-05_Graphics_in_Depth.pdf
