# Prepared archival research requests

These are ready-to-send research requests for evidence that is located but not
currently readable online. Sending a request and receiving a research copy are
external actions; no request is represented here as already sent.

## Stanford Special Collections — 1977 Apple booth photograph

**Collection:** Apple Computer, Inc. Records, M1007

**Primary target:** Box 10, Folder 12 — Apple timeline exhibit, “Apple Computer
booth, West Coast Computer Faire, 1977” photograph. [R3]

**Useful adjacent material:** Box 10, Folders 11, 13 and 15 (1977 Apple II
advertising/booth material).

**Stanford duplication policy:** remote research copies may be provided as PDF
or high-resolution TIFF; provision of a copy does not itself grant publication
permission. [R4]

### Draft request

> Subject: Research-copy request — Apple Computer M1007, 1977 West Coast Computer Faire booth
>
> I am researching the display hardware used by Apple during 1977, with a
> specific focus on whether a Sanyo VM-4209 or related monitor is visible in
> surviving Apple booth material. I would like to request the highest-resolution
> research reproduction available for Apple Computer, Inc. Records M1007,
> Box 10, Folder 12, described in the finding aid as “Apple Computer booth,
> West Coast Computer Faire, 1977.” If feasible, please preserve the full frame,
> edges, background equipment, and any captions/verso markings rather than
> supplying a cropped publication image.
>
> I would also appreciate availability/cost information for adjacent Box 10
> Folders 11, 13 and 15 if they contain display-identifying imagery or text.
> Please also advise what separate permission, if any, would be required to
> reproduce a research image in a public, noncommercial historical GitHub
> repository with full attribution.

Stanford contact listed by its duplication page: `specialcollections@stanford.edu`.

## Victoria & Albert Museum — Sanyo / Jobs trade-fair claim

A 2020 published interview with a V&A curator says that the museum collected an
Apple II alongside the same Sanyo monitor model Steve Jobs used to take to trade
fairs. [A8]

### Draft request

> Subject: Source enquiry — V&A Apple II Sanyo monitor and Steve Jobs trade-fair provenance
>
> I am researching early Apple display hardware and would like to document the
> evidentiary basis for the published V&A statement that its Apple II was
> collected with “the same model of the Sanyo monitor that Steve Jobs used to
> take to trade fairs.” Could the curatorial/accession record identify the Sanyo
> model number, the trade fair(s) and date(s), and the source used to establish
> that association (for example a period photograph, Apple record, oral history,
> or prior publication)?
>
> If a period image or accession photograph supports the identification, please
> also advise its catalog reference and the terms for reproducing it with
> attribution in a public, noncommercial research repository.

V&A Archive information: https://www.vam.ac.uk/info/va-archive

## National Diet Library — 1977/1978 Sanyo Technical Review

**Title:** 三洋電機技報 / *Sanyo Technical Review*

**NDL bibliographic ID:** 000000009550. [R2]

### Research objective

Search 1977–1978 contents/indexes for:

- `VM-4209`, `VM4209`, `4209`
- `VM-4092`, `VM4092`, `4092`
- ビデオモニター / video monitor
- データディスプレイ / data display
- industrial CCTV/data-display product announcements

### Draft request

> I am researching Sanyo professional monochrome video monitors of the 1976–78
> period, specifically VM-4092 and VM-4209. Please advise whether the 1977 and
> 1978 issues of 三洋電機技報 (Sanyo Technical Review, NDL bibliographic ID
> 000000009550) contain index entries, product notes, technical articles or
> advertisements for either model or for Sanyo video/data monitors. If relevant
> pages can be identified, I would like to request remote photocopy/PDF service
> for those pages and the issue cover/contents page needed to establish date and
> bibliographic identity.

Do not publish received scans until their reproduction terms are separately
reviewed.

## Smithsonian Archives Center — 1977 West Coast Computer Faire materials

The West Coast Computer Faire Collection, Series 1, contains 1977 product
literature, advertisements and handouts collected by an attendee. [R5]

### Research objective

Search the 1977 series for:

- Apple Computer booth or Apple literature;
- Byte Shop handouts/price lists;
- Sanyo industrial/computer monitor literature;
- monitor distributors whose literature identifies VM-4092 or VM-4209;
- photographs or exhibitor material with sufficiently visible monitor controls
  or labels to permit model identification.

Any requested image must receive a separate rights review before repository
redistribution.
