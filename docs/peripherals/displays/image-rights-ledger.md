# Image and document rights ledger

**Review date:** 2026-08-30

This ledger controls what visual material may be copied into the repository.
Historical importance is not a license. Buying a physical object or an eBay lot
does not normally transfer copyright in the seller's photographs.

## Smithsonian CC0 rule

The Smithsonian's Open Access program permits unrestricted reuse for items
specifically carrying a CC0 designation. The VM-4092 and VM-4209 object records
reviewed here instead display “Usage Conditions Apply.” They are therefore not
treated as CC0 material by this project.

## Public-domain advertising caution

Wikimedia Commons records the 1976 Apple-1 advertisement and December 1977
Apple II advertisement as public domain **in the United States** because the
advertisements were published without a separate copyright notice. Commons also
warns that the same work may remain protected in jurisdictions that do not apply
the U.S. rule in the same way. The files are retained here as U.S.-hosted
historical research material with that limitation stated explicitly.

## Owner-photo rule for the acquired VM-4209

When the monitor arrives, the preferred repository images are new photographs
made by the collection owner. Before adding them:

1. photograph the unopened shipping container and packing layers;
2. photograph all exterior faces before cleaning;
3. photograph front badge, controls, rear label, serial/date information,
   connectors, cable and any Apple sticker/marking separately;
4. retain original camera files and metadata;
5. record photographer, date, whether edits/crops were made, and chosen reuse
   license;
6. never treat a sticker or seller description as authenticated Apple provenance
   without separate evidence.
