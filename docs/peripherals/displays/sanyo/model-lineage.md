# Sanyo VM-4092 / VM-4209 model relationship

The available evidence does not support a simple “VM-4092 was replaced by
VM-4209” statement.

## What is established

- VM-4092 service literature exists from 1973. [S4]
- A Smithsonian VM-4092 was manufactured in February 1976. [S3]
- A VM-4209 is documented by surviving-specimen evidence in February 1977. [S1]
- Collector research reports VM-4092 and VM-4209 specimens with overlapping
  later dates, implying that the 4092 did not simply vanish when the 4209
  appeared. [S5]

## Working model

```text
SANYO PROFESSIONAL MONOCHROME MONITORS
             |
             +-- VM-4092 ---- documented before/through 1976 and later
             |
             +-- VM-4209 ---- documented from 1977; computer/data markets
```

`INFERENCE`: the models were overlapping products with some difference in
features, application, price, region, or market positioning.

`UNVERIFIED`: what Sanyo itself said the distinction was.

## Collector-observed differences

Applefritter discussion reports visible and internal differences, including a
power indicator associated with the VM-4209 and different chassis internals.
These observations are useful for directing archival research, but they are not
a substitute for Sanyo product literature. [S5]

## Rejected OEM shortcut

A vintage-computing mailing-list discussion considered whether VM-4209 might be
a rebadged Hitachi VM-909. After internal comparison, participants reported that
the machines were not the same internally. This hypothesis is therefore not
used in the project history. [S12]

## Needed primary evidence

A 1976–1978 Sanyo product catalog, price book, sales bulletin, or technical
article that lists both models would be the ideal source. The National Diet
Library holds digitized issues of *Sanyo Technical Review* across this period
and permits remote-copy requests, making it a concrete next research route.
