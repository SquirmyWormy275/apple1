# Sanyo VM-4092

**Research status:** evidence-reviewed through 2026-08-30.

The VM-4092 matters to Apple-1 history because it is demonstrably a 1976-era
Sanyo microcomputer display, whereas the earliest VM-4209 specimen located in
this research is dated February 1977.

## Confirmed chronology

| Evidence | Date | Strength |
|---|---:|---|
| Smithsonian holds a VM-4092 service manual dated 1973 | 1973 | `CONFIRMED-INSTITUTIONAL` |
| Smithsonian VM-4092 object label says manufactured February 1976; donor used it with an IMSAI 8080 | Feb. 1976 | `CONFIRMED-INSTITUTIONAL` |
| VM-4092 in an original-owner Apple-1 collection reported as June 1976 | June 1976 | `STRONG-PROVENANCE` |
| Winter 1977 computer catalog still advertises VM-4092 | 1977 | `CONFIRMED-PRIMARY` / catalog evidence |

Sources: [S3][S4][S5][S10].

## Apple-1 connection

Corey Cohen reports that the monitor associated with his 1976 Apple-1 is a
VM-4092 dated June 1976. The Apple-1 came from its original owner with other
period equipment associated with the Byte Shop purchase. Cohen does not present
a transaction document that separately prices the monitor and therefore uses
careful language: he considers it most likely purchased at about the same time.
[S5]

The appropriate catalog description is therefore:

> A June 1976 VM-4092 survives in a strong original-owner Apple-1 provenance
> chain. This does not prove that Byte Shop sold VM-4092s as an Apple-1 bundle.

## Why this matters

The Smithsonian's February 1976 object independently establishes the VM-4092 as
a genuine period microcomputer monitor. This removes the need to rely on Apple
collector lore to explain why a 1976 Apple-1 owner might use one. [S3]

## Relationship to VM-4209

Do not call the VM-4092 simply “the predecessor replaced by the VM-4209.”
Collector evidence includes later VM-4092 examples after VM-4209 production had
begun, so the products appear to have overlapped. The exact market distinction
has not been located in Sanyo factory literature. See [model-lineage.md](model-lineage.md).
