## Findings from the inventory

- No wholesale relocation is justified: the major domains already have coherent homes.
- The largest pre-v1 structural defect was navigation/authority discoverability, not directory placement.
- `cf-card/` versus `preservation/cf-card/`, current audit versus NEURAL1 audit, and overview versus detailed research agenda are intentional distinctions, not duplicates to delete.
- No preserved artifact, Pilot 001 evidence package, or unique branch lineage is a retirement candidate in this migration.
- Generated output remains ignored by default, with an explicit exception process for scientific evidence such as Pilot 001.
