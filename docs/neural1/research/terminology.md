# Operational terminology

- **Agent:** one logical identity with policy-isolated context.
- **Population:** agents evaluated under one declared schedule and policy.
- **Colony:** a population plus an independently evolving world state.
- **Generation:** one recorded scheduler cycle and selection transition.
- **Inheritance:** an artifact from an ancestor is present in a descendant's allowed initial state.
- **Mutation:** a recorded descendant difference from its parent artifact.
- **Migration:** an explicit artifact transfer between colonies.
- **Culture:** persistent, heritable software or documentation conventions detectable across context reset.
- **Civilization:** public shorthand for a colony with multi-generation culture; not a claim of consciousness.
- **Extinction:** no lineage in a declared population meets the survival criterion.
- **Convergence:** independently initialized lineages meet a predeclared structural/behavioral similarity rule.
- **Immigration/newcomer:** introduction of a previously unseen agent identity into established shared state.
