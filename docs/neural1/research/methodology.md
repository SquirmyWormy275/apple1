# Methodology

Pre-register factor levels, controls, seeds, primary metrics, analysis, sample
count, and stopping rule. Preserve prompts, provider records, world snapshots,
events, artifacts, and errors. Distinguish observation, matched comparison,
controlled replay, intervention, cross-seed replication, and cross-model
replication. A counterfactual should fork a snapshot and change one explicit
factor. Narrative speculation is labeled as such.

Model output is a proposal or interpretation. Assembler/emulator/runtime output
is deterministic evidence within its stated model. Physical claims require a
separately qualified live test.
