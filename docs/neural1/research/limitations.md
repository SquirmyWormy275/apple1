# Limitations

The world is not a full cycle-accurate Apple-1 emulator; bounded deposited-code
execution models only the NMOS CPU, allowed RAM, Monitor ECHO, and warm entry.
Local providers are implemented but not commissioned against installed models,
and final Pi benchmarks are uncommissioned. The experiment prototypes establish interfaces,
not emergent-culture findings. Historical component data is absent by design
until sources are curated. ASCII previews are not verified on the physical
display. No physical launch mechanism is claimed.
