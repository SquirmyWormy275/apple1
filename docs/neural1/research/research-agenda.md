# Research agenda

The flagship questions concern software inheritance under 4K-like limits,
historically plausible 1976 design space, staged self-hosting, monitor design
within exactly 256 bytes, and communication through shared RAM. Cross-layer
experiments may substitute evolved monitors or languages while changing one
declared factor. META/1 prioritizes questions by a published heuristic:
`priority = uncertainty + novelty + cross-layer relevance + expected information gain - normalized compute cost`.
Every component must be recorded; unbenchmarked compute cost remains unknown.
