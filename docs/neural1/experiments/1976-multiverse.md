# 1976 MULTIVERSE

`HistoricalComponent` records part identity, category, manufacturer,
availability evidence, source IDs, typed attributes, and authoritative status.
`MachineGenome` records parts, interconnections, memory map, timing assumptions,
and firmware identity. Deterministic validators reject unknown and unsourced
parts. Future cost, electrical, timing, and architecture evaluators must consume
source-backed records and expose uncertainty.

No authoritative historical parts/economics dataset is included. Test fixtures
are synthetic and cannot support claims about 1976 or the Apple-1. Blind search
must hide the Apple-1 genome until candidate generation ends.
