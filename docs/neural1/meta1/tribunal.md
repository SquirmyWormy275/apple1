# Tribunal and blind review

ADVOCATE assembles supporting records. SKEPTIC searches for selection bias,
seed artifacts, confounding, classification errors, model dependence,
survivorship bias, weak metrics, and post-hoc interpretation. REPLICATOR checks
the reproduction contract. EVIDENCE JUDGE applies declared deterministic rules
to records/statistics; another model vote is not adjudication. Blinding records
hidden fields, reviewer, timestamp, and reveal event.
