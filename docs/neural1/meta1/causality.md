# Causality

The ladder is: 0 observation; 1 matched comparison; 2 controlled replay; 3
removal/intervention; 4 cross-seed replication; 5 cross-model replication.
Statuses are UNKNOWN, OBSERVED, CORRELATED, INFERRED, and INTERVENTION SUPPORTED.
Higher replication broadens support but does not erase design flaws. `WHY`
reports the achieved level and alternatives; it never upgrades chronology into
causality.
