## Baseline result

Before NEURAL1 edits, 39 tests passed and one firmware-provenance test failed.
The cause is checkout line endings: `replica 110REV03.spin` and `Serial_IO.spin`
have LF working-tree bytes while their recorded SHA-256 values match CRLF bytes.
The other seven files match. The immutable source and manifest were not edited.
`.gitattributes` now declares CRLF checkout for those exact two paths so fresh
clones reproduce the recorded bytes; no firmware semantics or payload changed.

## Gaps intentionally retained

The repository does not establish the physical CFFA1 launch mechanism, a full
WozMon ROM emulator, arbitrary-code execution in the NEURAL1 world, physical
serial safety, final Pi/provider performance, authoritative 1976 component
economics, or redistribution permission for Apple1-Slideshow. Each gap has an
interface, fixture/test boundary, or commissioning path rather than a fabricated
answer.

## Safety audit

was written; no GPIO, jumper, solder, wiring, resistor, CA2, or camera action
