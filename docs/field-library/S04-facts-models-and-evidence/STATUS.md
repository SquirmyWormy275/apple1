## Expected result

A learner sorts three statements, then ten more, with reasoning. `ANSWERS.md`
gives a bin and a rationale for every item, and marks the two where a
well-argued alternative answer is acceptable.

## Known limitations

- The three-bin scheme is deliberately coarse. Real evidence work distinguishes
  more finely: direct observation, instrument capture, secondary report,
  inference. The repository's own evidence rules are the fuller version, and a
  STUDY-level learner should read `docs/preservation-dossier.md` next.
- Statement (b) is an open question in this project. If it is ever resolved,
  this lesson needs a different example, not a rewritten answer.

## Stop condition

Not applicable. No device interaction.

describes that record. It does not describe how to reproduce it, and per
`docs/preservation-dossier.md` an opened serial session remains blocked until a
measurement test card exists and an operator explicitly starts that single step.
