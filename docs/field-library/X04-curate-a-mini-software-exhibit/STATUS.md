## Expected result

Three artifacts chosen with distinct reasons, three cards with all nine fields
filled, and no blank cells in the rights audit. `ANSWERS.md` supplies a fully
worked three-card set plus field 9 for all eight available artifacts.

The intended insight is that the artifact most likely to be misread, the vendor
source archive, is the one worth putting in the middle of the set.

## Known limitations

- **This lesson resolves no rights or permission question.** It asks them. Rights
  status is unrecorded in this project for the vendor source archive, the manual
  scan, and the RAM-only programs (V-34), and this library cannot close that.
- Several fields in the worked cards read "not recorded" because the project
  genuinely does not record them. They were not invented.
- Part E item 8 records that this library's own lessons have not passed the
  curriculum's review gate and carry unverified page numbers. That is accurate and
  is included deliberately.

## Stop condition

Not applicable. No device interaction and no artifact handling.

## What this status does not authorize

No firmware load. No EEPROM write. No CFFA1 write. No serial-port open. No
physical modification.

**Including an artifact in an exhibit grants no authority to run, load, or
demonstrate it.** The RAM-only programs remain RAM-only candidates with no
live-run authority whether or not they appear on a card, and this lesson makes no
claim about the authenticity or value of anything.
