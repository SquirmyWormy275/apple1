## Expected result

Every item has one correct answer, keyed in `ANSWERS.md`. Part F is a guided
inference exercise with a model answer and an explicit instruction to mark
intent guesses as guesses.

## Known limitations

- Immediate and absolute addressing only, per the curriculum brief. Indexed
  addressing appears once, in a quoted real instruction, and is deferred to A02.
- Zero-page addressing is not named as a distinct mode.
- Seven mnemonics are covered, not the instruction set.

## Stop condition

Not applicable. No device interaction.

## What this status does not authorize

No firmware load. No EEPROM write. No CFFA1 write. No serial-port open. No
physical modification.
