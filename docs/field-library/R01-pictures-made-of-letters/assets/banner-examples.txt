WHAT MAKES ONE WORK:

  A LETTER NEEDS AT LEAST 5 ROWS
  TO BE READABLE.

  A GAP OF ONE COLUMN BETWEEN
  LETTERS IS TOO LITTLE.  USE TWO.

  FIVE LETTERS AT 5 WIDE PLUS 2
  GAPS IS 33 COLUMNS.  SIX LETTERS
  IS 40.  SEVEN DOES NOT FIT.

  SO PLAN THE WORD BEFORE YOU
  DRAW THE FIRST LETTER.
