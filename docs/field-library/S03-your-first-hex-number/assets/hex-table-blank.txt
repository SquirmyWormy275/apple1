APPLE-1 FIELD LIBRARY / S03
WORKSHEET: THE FIRST SIXTEEN

 DEC | HEX        DEC | HEX
-----+-----      -----+-----
  0  |             8  |
  1  |             9  |
  2  |            10  |
  3  |            11  |
  4  |            12  |
  5  |            13  |
  6  |            14  |
  7  |            15  |

ONE BYTE HOLDS TWO HEX DIGITS:

    $ _ _
      ^ ^
      | +-- LOW DIGIT  (0-15)
      +---- HIGH DIGIT (0-15)

SO A BYTE COUNTS $00 UP TO $FF.
