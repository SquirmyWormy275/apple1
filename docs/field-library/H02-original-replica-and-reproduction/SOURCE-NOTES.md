## The four definitions are not cited

"Original," "replica," "reproduction," and "restored" as defined on
`assets/word-card.txt` are working definitions written for this lesson. **No
source in this project defines them**, and usage varies across collecting fields
and between collectors.

The lesson says so where it matters: the replica/reproduction distinction is
described as soft and loosely used, and the Part C item 5 answer notes that
collectors disagree about heavily restored objects rather than presenting one
view as settled.

Recorded as **V-29**: the vocabulary in this lesson is a working scheme, not a
standard drawn from a cited authority. If this library is ever used alongside a
collecting community with its own conventions, the definitions should be checked
against them.

## No authentication content

This is the H02 brief's requirement, that the lesson be respectful of collectors
and make no authentication claims. What was done:

- **No object is authenticated anywhere in the packet.** All seven artifacts in
  Parts A and C are hypothetical and labeled as such.
- **No authentication method is taught.** Part B names the *kinds* of evidence a
  specialist would use, and Part F item 3 explicitly answers "partly," stating
  that the lesson does not teach how to evaluate them.
- **Part F item 2 answers "no"** to whether the lesson can say if a specific board
  is original, making the boundary an examinable item rather than a footnote.
- **No value or rarity claim appears** anywhere in the packet, and Part F item 4
  says so.

The correction letter in Part G is written to model the tone the brief asks for:
it assumes inherited error, offers a specific fix, and states that the writer has
not examined the object.

## Part E rests on the project's own record

The one-sentence description in the Part E answer deliberately marks nearly every
clause as needing evidence, including the manufacture date, which this library
cannot establish. It also carries forward **V-6**, the unresolved manual filename
discrepancy, rather than treating the manual's identity as settled.

## What this lesson does not establish or attempt

It authenticates nothing and teaches no authentication method. It makes no claim
about the value, rarity, or condition of any object. It authorizes no firmware
load, EEPROM write, CFFA1 write, serial-port open, or physical modification.
