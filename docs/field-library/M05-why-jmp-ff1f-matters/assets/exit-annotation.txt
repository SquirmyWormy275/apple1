APPLE-1 FIELD LIBRARY / M05
FINDING THE EXIT

PROGRAM ONE:  LINE-INPUT-0300.HEX

  0300  A0 00      LDY #$00
  0302  AD 11 D0   LDA $D011
  0305  10 FB      BPL $0302
  0307  AD 10 D0   LDA $D010
  030A  99 00 04   STA $0400,Y
  030D  20 EF FF   JSR $FFEF
  0310  C9 8D      CMP #$8D
  0312  F0 03      BEQ $0317
  0314  C8         INY
  0315  10 EB      BPL $0302
  0317  4C 1F FF   JMP $FF1F  <-EXIT

PROGRAM TWO:  LINE-INPUT-ECHO-0300.HEX
(LAST EIGHT INSTRUCTIONS ONLY)

  0317  A0 00      LDY #$00
  0319  B9 00 04   LDA $0400,Y
  031C  20 EF FF   JSR $FFEF
  031F  C9 8D      CMP #$8D
  0321  F0 03      BEQ $0326
  0323  C8         INY
  0324  10 F3      BPL $0319
  0326  4C 00 03   JMP $0300  <-LOOPS
                               BACK

TWO JUMPS.  ONE LEAVES.  ONE LOOPS.

WHY NOT RTS?

  JSR  PUSHES A RETURN ADDRESS,
       THEN JUMPS.
  RTS  PULLS A RETURN ADDRESS,
       THEN JUMPS TO IT.

  THEY COME AS A PAIR.

  THE MONITOR'S "R" COMMAND JUMPS.
  IT DOES NOT PUSH ANYTHING.

  SO AN RTS AT THE END PULLS
  SOMETHING THAT WAS NEVER PUT
  THERE, AND GOES TO WHATEVER
  ADDRESS THAT HAPPENS TO SPELL.
