## Expected result

Sections 1 to 6 of an author card, with an objective naming a watchable action
and a first result reachable in three actions or fewer. Parts B, C, and G have
determinate answers; the rest are keyed with acceptance criteria.

The intended insight in Part C is that "a program entered and running" fails as a
first result on authority grounds, not on the action count.

## Known limitations

- **A completed card is a proposal, not an approval.** It adds nothing to the
  catalog and grants nothing.
- Two card sections extend the curriculum's stated requirements: section 7's
  unsourced-claims prompt and section 12's wiring line (V-35). Both are consistent
  with existing project documents; neither is quoted from one.
- **The curriculum's review gate has not been formally applied to any packet in
  this library** (V-36). The answer key states this rather than holding a
  learner's proposal to a standard the catalog has not met.

## Stop condition

Not applicable. No device interaction.

## What this status does not authorize

No firmware load. No EEPROM write. No CFFA1 write. No serial-port open. No
physical modification.

**Section 12 is a gate, not a checklist: any yes means the lesson does not go in
the library**, and that applies to describing those actions as well as to
instructing them. A completed author card creates no authority, and any lesson
proposed through it remains subject to the curriculum's review gate.
