## Claims used

- Apple's 1976 Operation Manual documents generic composite-monitor use, TV use
  through an RF modulator, and names Motorola and Ball as example monitor makers.
- Smithsonian records a VM-4092 manufactured February 1976 and used with an
  IMSAI 8080.
- Current specimen evidence places VM-4209 by February 1977.
- BYTE explicitly lists VM4209 by January 1978 in the audited source register.
- A surviving April 1978 VM-4209 is documented with Apple property markings.
- The Apple-1 Registry describes the later auction-driven VM-4209/4509 visual
  association.
- The Henry Ford transcribes its ensemble VM-4209 label as manufactured June
  1979.

Use the source IDs and external URLs in `../../peripherals/displays/sources.md`
rather than duplicating the entire bibliography here.
