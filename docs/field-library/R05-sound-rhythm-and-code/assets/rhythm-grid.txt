THE RHYTHM GRID

A RHYTHM IS A ROW OF SLOTS.
EACH SLOT IS ON OR OFF.

  1 2 3 4 5 6 7 8
  X . . . X . . .     CLAP ON 1 AND 5

  1 2 3 4 5 6 7 8
  X . X . X . X .     CLAP ON EVERY
                      OTHER SLOT

  1 2 3 4 5 6 7 8
  X . . X . . X .     CLAP ON 1, 4, 7

READ EACH ROW OUT LOUD.
COUNT 1 TO 8 STEADILY AND CLAP ON
THE X.

NOW ENCODE THEM AS BITS.
X IS 1.  DOT IS 0.

  X . . . X . . .  =  1000 1000  = $88

  X . X . X . X .  =  ________  =  ___

  X . . X . . X .  =  ________  =  ___

ONE BYTE HOLDS AN EIGHT-SLOT
RHYTHM.  THAT IS THE WHOLE
REPRESENTATION.

NOW WRITE YOUR OWN:

  1 2 3 4 5 6 7 8
  _ _ _ _ _ _ _ _  =  ________  =  ___

CLAP IT.  THEN HAND THE BYTE TO
SOMEONE ELSE AND SEE IF THEY CLAP
THE SAME THING.

WHAT THE BYTE DOES NOT SAY:

  HOW FAST      ______________
  HOW LOUD      ______________
  HOW LONG EACH ______________
  WHAT SOUND    ______________
