EVIDENCE MODE: CFFA1_FIRMWARE

THE RAM READER USES THE REAL CFFA1 API
AND A MODELED KEYBOARD/DISPLAY. THE
REHEARSAL CHECKS FIRST PAGE, NEXT,
PREVIOUS, ANSWER JUMP, BOOK SELECTION
AND MONITOR EXIT. THESE ARE CPU
RESULTS, NOT PHYSICAL SCREEN
PHOTOGRAPHS.

TRY: WHAT HAPPENS IF THE READER IS
LAUNCHED WITH THE WRONG PREFIX?

CHECK: IT SHOWS A CFFA ERROR AND
OFFERS ANOTHER BOOK SELECTION. CHOOSE
EDUCATION IN THE CFFA MENU BEFORE
LOADING THE READER.
