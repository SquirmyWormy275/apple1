C05 INSTRUCTIONS CHANGE STATE
030/033 ANSWERS

0302:  8D 01 04  STA $0401
0305:  4C 1F FF  JMP $FF1F

THAT IS THREE INSTRUCTIONS, WHICH IS
FINE; FOUR WAS A CEILING, NOT A QUOTA.
A FOUR-INSTRUCTION VERSION MIGHT ADD
CLC OR LOAD AND ADD.

ONE THING IT ASSUMES BUT DOES NOT
ESTABLISH: THAT $0401 IS WRITABLE RAM
ON WHATEVER MACHINE RUNS IT. THE
PROGRAM HAS NO WAY TO CHECK, AND A
STORE TO ROM OR TO AN UNMAPPED ADDRESS
WOULD SILENTLY DO NOTHING.

ACCEPT ANY HONEST ANSWER OF THAT
SHAPE. REJECT "IT ASSUMES NOTHING."
