EVIDENCE MODE: SYNTHETIC_SOFTWARE

A DIRECTORY WITH A FAMILIAR NAME IS
NOT NECESSARILY THE COMMISSIONED
VOLUME. THIS EXAMPLE INVOKES THE
ACTUAL STORAGE VERIFICATION GUARD WITH
A FRESHLY CREATED ORDINARY DIRECTORY.
REJECTION IS THE EXPECTED SUCCESS OF
THE GUARD.

TRY: WHY CHECK A FILESYSTEM IDENTITY
RATHER THAN ONLY FREE SPACE?

CHECK: A MISSING MOUNT CAN LEAVE AN
ORDINARY DIRECTORY ON ANOTHER DISK.
PLENTY OF FREE SPACE ON THE WRONG DISK
DOES NOT MAKE THE DESTINATION CORRECT.
