036/055 F24 COPY AND VERIFY A MIGRATIO

EVIDENCE MODE: SYNTHETIC_SOFTWARE

THE STORAGE WORKFLOW SEPARATES
PLANNING, COPYING, AND VERIFICATION.
THIS EXAMPLE CREATES ITS OWN MARKED
TEMPORARY DIRECTORIES AND TINY FIXTURE
FILE. IT DOES NOT IDENTIFY A REAL SSD,
CHANGE MOUNTS, FORMAT A DEVICE, OR
FINALIZE DELETION OF USER DATA.

TRY: WHY NOT FINALIZE DELETION AS THE
FIRST TEACHING STEP?

CHECK: VERIFICATION AND AN INDEPENDENT
RETAINED COPY COME FIRST. FINALIZATION
IS A SEPARATELY GUARDED OPERATOR
ACTION, NOT A REQUIREMENT FOR LEARNING
COPY INTEGRITY.
