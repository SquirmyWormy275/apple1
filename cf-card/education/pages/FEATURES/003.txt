EVIDENCE MODE: SYNTHETIC_SOFTWARE

A RECORDING BINDS A RESPONSE TO ITS
PROMPT, AGENT, AND SEED. EXACT REPLAY
MUST REFUSE A MISSING TUPLE RATHER
THAN SUPPLY A PLAUSIBLE REPLACEMENT.
THE PROVIDER BELOW IS DELIBERATELY
FAKE, SO THIS TESTS RECORDING BEHAVIOR
WITHOUT INFERENCE.

TRY: WOULD FIXING ONLY THE RANDOM SEED
REPRODUCE A DIFFERENT MODEL BUILD?

CHECK: NO. PRESERVE WEIGHTS/MANIFEST
IDENTITY, CONFIGURATION, PROMPT,
RUNTIME, AND RECORDINGS. A SEED IS
ONLY ONE INPUT.
