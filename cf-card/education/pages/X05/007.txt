WRITING OFF-DEVICE IS NOT A FORMALITY.
IT IS A STATEMENT THAT A LEARNER CAN
COMPLETE YOUR LESSON WITHOUT GOING
NEAR THE MACHINE, AND IF THEY CANNOT,
THE LESSON NEEDS REDESIGNING RATHER
THAN RELABELLING.

EVERY QUESTION NEEDS AN ANSWER,
INCLUDING THE OPEN-ENDED ONES. SECTION
9 ASKS HOW MANY QUESTIONS AND HOW THE
OPEN-ENDED ONES WILL BE JUDGED. "IT
DEPENDS" IS NOT AN ANSWER KEY. WHAT
THE EXISTING LESSONS DO IS SUPPLY A
WORKED EXAMPLE PLUS EXPLICIT
ACCEPTANCE CRITERIA, SO AN EDUCATOR
CAN JUDGE TWO DIFFERENT LEARNERS
CONSISTENTLY.
