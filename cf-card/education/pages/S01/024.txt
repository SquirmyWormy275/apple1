THE HARDER ONE IS #4. BOTH THE
ORIGINAL BOARD AND THE REPLICA START
THEIR MONITOR AT RESET, SO LEARNERS
OFTEN ANSWER B OR R. THE STATEMENT IS
STILL ABOUT THE MONITOR, BECAUSE THE
MONITOR IS THE THING THAT RUNS. THE
BOARD AND THE REPLICA ARE THE THINGS
IT RUNS *ON*. IF A LEARNER ARGUED FOR
B OR R AND CAN STATE THAT DISTINCTION,
THEY HAVE UNDERSTOOD THE LESSON.
