M02 ADDRESSES AND BYTE ENTRY
023/032 ANSWERS

 /  $0300  /  1  /  $0300  /

THE LAST ROW IS THE CHECK ON THE RULE:
ONE BYTE OCCUPIES EXACTLY THE LOAD
ADDRESS AND NOTHING FURTHER.

PART C: WHICH BYTE IS WHERE

 /  ADDRESS  /  BYTE  /
 /  $0300  /  A0  /
 /  $0305  /  10  /
 /  $030D  /  20  /
 /  $0317  /  4C  /
 /  $0319  /  FF  /

COUNTING TIP: $030D IS THE 14TH BYTE,
BECAUSE $0D IS 13 AND THE FIRST BYTE
IS AT OFFSET 0.
