THE CHARACTER AFTER THE MNEMONIC
CHANGES THE MEANING COMPLETELY:

- LDA #$00 HAS A #. THE # MEANS
*IMMEDIATE*: THE VALUE ITSELF. LOAD
THE NUMBER ZERO.
- LDA $D011 HAS NO #. IT MEANS
*ABSOLUTE*: AN ADDRESS. GO TO $D011
AND LOAD WHATEVER IS SITTING THERE.

THAT SINGLE CHARACTER IS THE
DIFFERENCE BETWEEN THE NUMBER 53 AND
THE CONTENTS OF HOUSE NUMBER 53.
