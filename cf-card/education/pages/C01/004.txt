WHAT THE CPU IS. THE APPLE-1 USES A
MOS 6502, A *MICROPROCESSOR*: ONE CHIP
THAT READS INSTRUCTIONS AND CARRIES
THEM OUT. IT HAS ALMOST NO MEMORY OF
ITS OWN. WHAT IT HAS IS A HANDFUL OF
*REGISTERS*, TINY NAMED STORAGE
LOCATIONS INSIDE THE CHIP, EACH
HOLDING ONE BYTE.
