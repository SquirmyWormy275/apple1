PART E: THE DOUBLE FRAME

1. OPTION A SHOWS THE BALL AT 17
TWICE; OPTION B SHOWS 17 THEN 13.
2. OPTION A LOOKS LIKE A PAUSE. THE
BALL APPEARS TO REST AGAINST THE WALL
FOR A MOMENT.
3. OPTION B LOOKS SHARPER. THE
REVERSAL IS INSTANT.
4. NEITHER IS WRONG. A BALL HITTING A
HARD WALL MIGHT LOOK BETTER SHARP; A
BALL HITTING SOMETHING SOFT MIGHT LOOK
BETTER WITH THE PAUSE. IT IS A DESIGN
DECISION, AND IT ONLY BECOMES A BUG IF
IT HAPPENS BY ACCIDENT.
