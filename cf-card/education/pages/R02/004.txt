THE RULE IS A CHANGE, NOT A PICTURE.
FRAMES 1 TO 3 ARE NOT THREE DRAWINGS
SOMEONE MADE SEPARATELY. THEY ARE ONE
DRAWING PLUS A RULE: MOVE FOUR COLUMNS
RIGHT. ONCE YOU HAVE THE RULE YOU CAN
GENERATE ANY NUMBER OF FRAMES WITHOUT
DRAWING THEM.

THAT IS THE SHIFT WORTH MAKING. AN
ANIMATION IS NOT A COLLECTION OF
PICTURES. IT IS A STARTING PICTURE AND
A RULE FOR THE NEXT ONE.

STATE IS WHAT HAS TO BE REMEMBERED. TO
PRODUCE THE NEXT FRAME YOU NEED TO
KNOW WHERE THE BALL IS NOW. THAT IS
ONE NUMBER: THE POSITION. WHEN THE
BALL HAS TO BOUNCE, YOU ALSO NEED TO
