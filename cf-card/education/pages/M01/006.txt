NO FILESYSTEM. IT DOES NOT TRACK WHAT
IS RUNNING, CANNOT STOP A PROGRAM, AND
OFFERS NO WAY TO UNDO A CHANGE. IT IS
CLOSER TO A PAIR OF TWEEZERS THAN TO
WINDOWS.
