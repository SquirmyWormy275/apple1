BEFORE MACHINES LIKE THIS, USING A
COMPUTER MEANT SETTING ROWS OF
PHYSICAL SWITCHES TO ENTER EACH BYTE
AND READING LIGHTS TO SEE THE RESULT.
THE ALTAIR WORKED THAT WAY. THE
MONITOR REPLACED THE SWITCHES WITH
TYPING.

THE THREE JOBS.

THE MONITOR'S THREE BASIC FUNCTIONS
ARE INSPECT MEMORY, CHANGE MEMORY, AND
RUN A PROGRAM. THE BRIEL MANUAL CALLS
THESE EXAMINE, PROGRAM, AND RUN
(R-MON-3). HERE, "PROGRAM" MEANS
DEPOSITING BYTES INTO MEMORY.
