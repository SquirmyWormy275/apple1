B01 FROM INSTRUCTIONS TO BASIC
030/033 ANSWERS


THREE THINGS THE ASSEMBLY VERSION CAN
DO THAT INPUT CANNOT:

1. STORE THE CHARACTERS AT AN ADDRESS
OF YOUR CHOOSING, $0400.
2. REACT TO EACH CHARACTER AS IT
ARRIVES RATHER THAN WAITING FOR THE
WHOLE LINE.
3. RUN WITHOUT BASIC BEING PRESENT AT
ALL.

TWO THINGS INPUT DOES THAT THE
ASSEMBLY VERSION DOES NOT:

1. PRINTS A PROMPT, AND THE AUTOMATIC
QUESTION MARK.
2. MAKES THE RESULT AVAILABLE AS A
N NEXT P BACK A ANSWER M BOOK Q EXIT
