OPERATOR NAMES EXPLICITLY. IT DOES NOT
CRAWL, COPY, OR PUBLISH. BESIDE EACH

HUMAN NOTE RECORDING ORIGINAL
LOCATION, SOURCE, PERMISSION, DATE,
AND WHETHER THE FILE IS AN ORIGINAL, A
DERIVATIVE, OR A WORKING COPY.
