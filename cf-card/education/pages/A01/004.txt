THE MNEMONICS ARE ABBREVIATED ENGLISH.
LDA IS LOAD ACCUMULATOR. STA IS STORE
ACCUMULATOR. LDX AND LDY LOAD THE
INDEX REGISTERS, STX AND STY STORE
THEM. ONCE YOU SEE THE PATTERN, MOST
OF THE INSTRUCTION SET READS ITSELF.

LOAD AND STORE ARE OPPOSITES, AND THE
DIRECTION MATTERS. LDA $0400 READS
FROM MEMORY INTO THE REGISTER. STA
$0400 WRITES FROM THE REGISTER INTO
MEMORY. THE REGISTER IS ALWAYS THE *A*
IN THE MNEMONIC, AND THE OPERAND IS
