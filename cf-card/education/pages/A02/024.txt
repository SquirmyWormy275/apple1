024/029 ANSWERS

PART F: THE COLLISION

1. $0300 TO $0319. 26 BYTES FROM
$0300.
2. $0310 IS INSIDE THE PROGRAM.
WRITING A BUFFER THERE OVERWRITES THE
PROGRAM'S OWN INSTRUCTIONS WHILE IT IS
RUNNING.
3. ALMOST ANYTHING. THE PROGRAM MIGHT
WORK FOR A FEW CHARACTERS AND THEN
BEHAVE STRANGELY, OR JUMP SOMEWHERE
MEANINGLESS, OR STOP RESPONDING,
BECAUSE THE BYTES IT IS ABOUT TO
EXECUTE HAVE BEEN REPLACED WITH TEXT.
THE SYMPTOM WOULD LOOK NOTHING LIKE
"THE BUFFER IS IN THE WRONG PLACE,"
WHICH IS WHAT MAKES THIS CLASS OF BUG
EXPENSIVE.
