$0400 IS CALLED THE *BASE ADDRESS* AND
Y IS THE *INDEX*. THE CORRESPONDING
LOAD, LDA $0400,Y, READS BACK FROM THE
SAME PLACE, WHICH IS EXACTLY WHAT THE
ECHO PROGRAM USES TO READ THE BUFFER
OUT AGAIN.

LABELS ARE NOT ADDRESSES. IN A LISTING
YOU WILL SEE THINGS LIKE:

FF1F: A9 8D GETLINE LDA #$8D

GETLINE IS A *LABEL*, A NAME A HUMAN
WROTE FOR THE BENEFIT OF OTHER HUMANS.
IT IS NOT IN THE BYTES. THE
INSTRUCTION IS A9 8D AND THE ADDRESS
IS $FF1F; GETLINE EXISTS ONLY IN THE
