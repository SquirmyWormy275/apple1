014/015 L08 USE NEGATIVE CONTROLS BEFO

EVIDENCE MODE: VIRTUAL_EXECUTION

A USEFUL EVALUATOR MUST DISTINGUISH A
CANDIDATE THAT MEETS THE TASK FROM ONE
THAT DOES NOT. THIS LESSON USES THE
EXACT-256 EVALUATOR WITH A POSITIVE
AND A NEGATIVE AUTHORED FIXTURE, SO
"THE EVALUATOR RAN" CANNOT SUBSTITUTE
FOR "THE TASK PASSED."

TRY: DOES PASSING A POSITIVE CONTROL
ESTABLISH A GENERAL REPLACEMENT
MONITOR?

CHECK: NO. IT ESTABLISHES THAT
SPECIFIC OUTPUT/RETURN TASK UNDER ITS
EXPLICIT CONSTRAINTS.
