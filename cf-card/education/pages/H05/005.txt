005/030 LEARN

TELLS A FUTURE READER, POSSIBLY YOU,
WHERE TO BE CAREFUL. A CONFIDENT LOG
INVITES CONFIDENT CONCLUSIONS. A LOG
THAT SAYS "I COULD NOT TELL WHETHER
THE FLICKER WAS THE MONITOR OR THE
BOARD" HANDS OVER SOMETHING GENUINELY
USEFUL.

LOG THE DAY NOTHING HAPPENED. THIS
SOUNDS LIKE BUSYWORK AND IS NOT. A GAP
IN A LOG IS AMBIGUOUS: DID NOTHING
HAPPEN, OR DID SOMEBODY FORGET TO
WRITE? AN ENTRY SAYING "READ TWO
LESSONS, POWERED NOTHING ON" REMOVES
THAT AMBIGUITY FOR THE COST OF ONE
LINE.

LOG WHAT YOU DECIDED NOT TO DO.
