M05 WHY JMP $FF1F MATTERS
027/033 ANSWERS

REPOSITORY'S SOFTWARE LIBRARY
DESCRIBES IT AS READING THE BUFFER
BACK "BEFORE STARTING OVER," WHICH IS
A DESCRIPTION OF A LOOP. SO THE
BEHAVIOR MAY BE EXACTLY AS INTENDED
AND THE GENERAL RULE IN THE RAM-ONLY
README MAY SIMPLY NOT HAVE BEEN
WRITTEN WITH THIS PROGRAM'S RESTARTING
DESIGN IN MIND.

WHAT WOULD SETTLE IT: A STATEMENT FROM
WHOEVER WROTE OR ADOPTED THE ARTIFACT
ABOUT WHETHER THE RESTART IS
INTENTIONAL. THAT IS A QUESTION FOR
THE REPOSITORY OWNER, NOT SOMETHING A
LEARNER CAN RESOLVE BY READING BYTES.
