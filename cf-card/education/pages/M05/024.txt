IF A PROGRAM STARTED WITH R ENDS WITH
RTS, IT JUMPS TO WHATEVER TWO BYTES
HAPPEN TO BE ON THE STACK. NOTHING WAS
PUT THERE FOR IT. THOSE BYTES ARE
LEFTOVERS, AND THEIR VALUE IS NOT
PREDICTABLE FROM THE PROGRAM.
EXECUTION CONTINUES AT THAT ADDRESS,
INTERPRETING WHATEVER IS THERE AS
INSTRUCTIONS.

THE HONEST ANSWER TO "WHAT ADDRESS"
IS: UNKNOWABLE FROM THE PROGRAM ALONE.
