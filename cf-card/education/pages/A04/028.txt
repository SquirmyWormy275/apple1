JUMP. A CORRECTED VERSION NEEDS TWO
JUMPS FOR THREE PATHS.

THE GENERAL RULE: N PATHS REJOINING
NEED N MINUS 1 JUMPS, BECAUSE EXACTLY
ONE PATH CAN BE ADJACENT TO THE ENDING
AND FALL THROUGH. ACCEPT ANY WORKING
VERSION; THE ANSWER WORTH HAVING IS
THE RULE.

NOTE ALSO D0 04 USED AS AN
ALWAYS-TAKEN BRANCH AT $0312: AFTER
LDA #$BF THE ZERO FLAG IS CLEAR
BECAUSE $BF IS NOT ZERO, SO BNE ALWAYS
BRANCHES. THIS IS A REAL TECHNIQUE AND
A GOOD THING TO NOTICE, BUT IT IS
FRAGILE, AND JMP SAYS WHAT IT MEANS.
