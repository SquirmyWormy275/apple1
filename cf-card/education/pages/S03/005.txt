S03 YOUR FIRST HEX NUMBER
005/025 LEARN

COMPUTER MEMORY IS BUILT OUT OF BITS,
AND BITS COME IN GROUPS OF EIGHT.
EIGHT BITS IS A *BYTE*. A BYTE CAN
HOLD 256 DIFFERENT VALUES, 0 THROUGH
255. SIXTEEN TIMES SIXTEEN IS 256, SO
EXACTLY TWO HEX DIGITS DESCRIBE
EXACTLY ONE BYTE, EVERY TIME, WITH NO
LEFTOVERS. TEN DOES NOT DIVIDE INTO
THAT CLEANLY: 255 IN DECIMAL IS THREE
DIGITS, 99 IS TWO, AND THERE IS NO
FIXED WIDTH.

THAT IS THE WHOLE REASON. HEX IS NOT
MORE MATHEMATICAL THAN DECIMAL. IT
JUST LINES UP WITH THE HARDWARE, SO A
BYTE IS ALWAYS TWO CHARACTERS AND AN
ADDRESS IS ALWAYS FOUR.
