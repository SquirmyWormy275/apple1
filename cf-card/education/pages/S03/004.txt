COUNTING IN SIXTEENS. HEXADECIMAL, OR
*HEX*, USES SIXTEEN SYMBOLS. THE FIRST
TEN ARE THE FAMILIAR 0 TO 9. SINCE
THERE ARE NO SINGLE DIGITS FOR TEN
THROUGH FIFTEEN, LETTERS DO THE JOB: A
IS 10, B IS 11, C IS 12, D IS 13, E IS
14, F IS
15. AFTER F COMES $10, MEANING "ONE
GROUP OF SIXTEEN, NO ONES," WHICH IS
DECIMAL 16.

THE $ PREFIX MEANS "THIS IS HEX." YOU
WILL ALSO SEE 0X USED FOR THE SAME
PURPOSE. WITHOUT A MARKER, 10 IS
AMBIGUOUS: SIXTEEN OR TEN?
