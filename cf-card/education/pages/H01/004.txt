THE APPLE-1 INTEGRATED A KEYBOARD
INTERFACE AND TEXT-VIDEO OUTPUT ON THE
BOARD. THAT MADE TYPING AND READING
THE NORMAL INTERACTION RATHER THAN
USING A SWITCH PANEL FOR ORDINARY
PROGRAM ENTRY. OWAD CALLS IT THE FIRST
COMPUTER "OF ITS KIND" WITH THIS
ARRANGEMENT. THAT PHRASE LEAVES THE
COMPARISON CLASS UNDEFINED, SO THIS
LESSON TREATS IT AS THE AUTHOR'S
CHARACTERIZATION, NOT A SETTLED
PRIORITY CLAIM. EARLIER TERMINALS AND
OTHER COMPUTERS EXISTED.

THE DOCUMENTED INTERFACE IS THE USEFUL
ENGINEERING DISTINCTION HERE. IT DOES
NOT ESTABLISH THAT THE APPLE-1 WAS THE
