BY 1977 THERE WAS AN OWNERS' CLUB WITH
ABOUT THIRTY MEMBERS, FOUNDED BY JOE
TORZEWSKI, EXCHANGING PROGRAMS AND
WORKING OUT HOW TO EXPAND THE MEMORY.
APPLE SENT ITS APPLE-1 CUSTOMERS TO
THE CLUB FOR SUPPORT. THIRTY PEOPLE IS
A SMALL NUMBER, AND IT WAS ENOUGH TO
KEEP THE MACHINE'S KNOWLEDGE ALIVE FOR
DECADES.

AND THEN IT WAS OVER QUICKLY. THE
APPLE II ARRIVED AND ATTENTION MOVED.
ONE DEALER'S ACCOUNT DESCRIBES THE
APPLE-1 MARKET DRYING UP FAST ONCE THE
II WAS INTRODUCED. THE UNSOLD BOARD
THAT SAT IN HIS DISPLAY CASE BECAME
INTERESTING AGAIN ONLY MUCH LATER; HE
AUCTIONED IT IN 2001, TWENTY-FIVE
