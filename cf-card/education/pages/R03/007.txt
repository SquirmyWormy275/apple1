WHERE THE ARITHMETIC GETS AWKWARD ON
THIS MACHINE. "DIVIDES EXACTLY BY" IS
EASY TO SAY AND IS DIVISION, WHICH THE
6502 DOES NOT HAVE AS AN INSTRUCTION.
REPEATED SUBTRACTION OR A COUNTER THAT
RESETS IS HOW YOU WOULD ACTUALLY DO
IT. RULES BASED ON COUNTING AND
RESETTING ARE MUCH CHEAPER THAN RULES
BASED ON DIVISION, WHICH IS THE KIND
OF CONSIDERATION THAT SHAPES WHAT
PATTERNS ARE PRACTICAL RATHER THAN
MERELY POSSIBLE.
