R03 GENERATIVE PATTERNS
032/036 ANSWERS

1. ADD ONE AND SUBTRACT, SINGLE
INSTRUCTIONS.
2. COMPARE, A SINGLE INSTRUCTION.
3. MULTIPLY BY TWO, A SINGLE SHIFT
INSTRUCTION.
4. DIVIDE, WHICH THE 6502 HAS NO
INSTRUCTION FOR AT ALL. IT MUST BE
BUILT FROM REPEATED SUBTRACTION OR
SHIFTING.

WHAT THAT MAKES PRACTICAL: RULES BASED
ON COUNTING AND RESETTING A COUNTER.
"EVERY THIRD COLUMN" IS CHEAP IF YOU
KEEP A COUNTER THAT COUNTS 1, 2, 3,
RESETS, AND PRINT WHEN IT RESETS. THE
SAME RULE STATED AS "WHERE THE COLUMN
DIVIDES BY 3" SOUNDS LIKE DIVISION AND
IS NOT, ONCE YOU WRITE IT THAT WAY.
