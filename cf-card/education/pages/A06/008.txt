*BIGGEST* IS THE BOUNDARY: THE FULL
BUFFER, THE LAST ADDRESS, THE LARGEST
VALUE. A05'S 128-CHARACTER LIMIT LIVES
HERE.

*WRONG* IS THE INPUT YOU DID NOT
DESIGN FOR: A KEY YOU WERE NOT
EXPECTING, A CONTROL CHARACTER, A
VALUE OUT OF RANGE.

"WHAT IT DOES NOT DO" IS A FEATURE OF
THE DESIGN, NOT AN APOLOGY. A05'S
PROGRAM HAS NO BACKSPACE, AND THAT IS
A LEGITIMATE CHOICE FOR 26 BYTES.
WRITING THE OMISSION DOWN MEANS IT WAS
DECIDED RATHER THAN OVERLOOKED, AND IT
TELLS THE NEXT PERSON READING THE CODE
