LOWERCASE ASCII LETTER, CLEARING BIT 5
CONVERTS IT TO UPPERCASE. A GENERAL
INPUT ROUTINE NEEDS RANGE CHECKS
BEFORE THAT OPERATION. APPLYING AND
#$DF BLINDLY ALSO CHANGES OTHER
CHARACTERS: ASCII 0 ($30) BECOMES
CONTROL CODE $10.

COMPARE THE COMPLETE POLICIES:
PRESERVE THE ORIGINAL, CONVERT LETTERS
AFTER CHECKING THE RANGE, OR REJECT
UNSUPPORTED INPUT VISIBLY. CONVERSION
SILENTLY CHANGES THE USER'S TEXT, SO
IT SHOULD BE A DOCUMENTED CHOICE.
INSTRUCTION COST DEPENDS ON THOSE
CHECKS; ONE AND INSTRUCTION IS NOT A
COMPLETE GENERAL CONVERTER.
