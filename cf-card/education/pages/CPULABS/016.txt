CONTRIBUTES 1 INSTRUCTION, EACH
ORDINARY PASS CONTRIBUTES 10, THE CR
PASS CONTRIBUTES 8, AND THE FINAL JMP
CONTRIBUTES 1. THUS 10*(N+1). WITH 128
ORDINARY CHARACTERS, Y REACHES $80
BEFORE THE SUPPLIED CR IS READ, GIVING
1282 INSTRUCTIONS AND A BUFFER WITH NO
CR. THE HARNESS READS A MAXIMUM 128
BUFFER BYTES.

CHARACTERS FOLLOWED BY RETURN?
