011/019 T08 TWO KINDS OF INDEXED POINT

EVIDENCE MODE: VIRTUAL_EXECUTION

A ZERO-PAGE POINTER OCCUPIES A LOW
BYTE FOLLOWED BY A HIGH BYTE. ($20),Y
READS THAT POINTER THEN ADDS Y;
($20,X) ADDS X WITHIN ZERO PAGE BEFORE
READING A POINTER. THE FIRST EXAMPLE
DELIBERATELY CROSSES $04FF TO $0500.
THESE ARE NMOS CPU FORMS, AND THEIR
ALLOWED USE STILL DEPENDS ON THE
SURROUNDING EXPERIMENT POLICY.

TRY: SET Y=0 IN THE FIRST INDIRECT
LOAD. DOES IT STILL READ $0500?

CHECK: NO. THE POINTER IS $04FF, SO
WITHOUT THE INCREMENT IT READS $04FF,
WHICH THIS FIXTURE LEAVES ZERO.
