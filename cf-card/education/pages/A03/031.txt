PART G: COUNT UP

ONE CORRECT ANSWER:

0300  A2 00     LDX #$00
0302  E8        INX
0303  E0 05     CPX #$05
0305  D0 FB     BNE $0302
0307  8E 00 04  STX $0400
030A  4C 1F FF  JMP $FF1F

FLAG TESTED: THE ZERO FLAG, VIA BNE,
BECAUSE THE STOPPING CONDITION IS "X
HAS REACHED 5," AND CPX #$05 SETS THE
ZERO FLAG EXACTLY WHEN THAT IS TRUE.
