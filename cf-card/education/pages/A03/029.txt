A03 COUNTING AND LOOPING
029/034 ANSWERS

PART F: FIND THE OFF-BY-ONE

1. BRANCH TARGET: $030A + 2 + (-8) =
$0304. $F8 IS 248, AND 248 MINUS 256
IS -8.
2. IT STORES FIVE CHARACTERS, AT $0400
THROUGH $0404.
3. THERE IS NO BUG. THE LOOP IS
CORRECT.

TRACE IT: Y STARTS 0, STORES AT $0400,
INY MAKES Y 1, CPY #$05 IS NOT EQUAL,
BRANCH BACK. THIS REPEATS FOR Y = 1,
2, 3, 4. AFTER STORING AT $0404, INY
MAKES Y 5, CPY #$05 IS EQUAL, THE ZERO
FLAG IS SET, BNE DOES NOT BRANCH, AND
CONTROL FALLS THROUGH.
