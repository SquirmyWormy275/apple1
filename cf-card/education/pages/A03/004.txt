BRANCHES TEST A FLAG, NOT A VALUE. DEX
DOES NOT JUST CHANGE X; IT ALSO SETS
FLAGS DESCRIBING THE RESULT. THE ZERO
FLAG IS SET WHEN THE RESULT WAS ZERO.
THE NEGATIVE FLAG IS SET WHEN BIT 7 OF
THE RESULT WAS 1. BNE LOOKS AT THE
ZERO FLAG, BPL LOOKS AT THE NEGATIVE
FLAG. NEITHER ONE LOOKS AT X DIRECTLY,
WHICH IS WHY A BRANCH CAN FOLLOW AN
INSTRUCTION THAT TOUCHED A DIFFERENT
REGISTER ENTIRELY.
