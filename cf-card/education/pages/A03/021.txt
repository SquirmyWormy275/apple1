HOW A BRANCH FINDS ITS TARGET

A BRANCH IS TWO BYTES:

    D0 FD
    !   +-- THE OFFSET
    +------ THE BRANCH OPCODE

THE OFFSET IS COUNTED FROM THE
INSTRUCTION AFTER THE BRANCH.

  0303  D0 FD
  0305  <- COUNTING STARTS HERE

  $FD AS A SIGNED BYTE IS -3.

  0305 - 3 = 0302.  TARGET.
