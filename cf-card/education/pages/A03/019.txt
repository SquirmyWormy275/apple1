A03 COUNTING AND LOOPING
019/034 WORKSHEET COUNTDOWN-TRACE.TXT


  DEX RAN ______ TIMES.
  BRANCH TAKEN ______ TIMES.
  FINAL X = ______
  $0400 = ______


PROGRAM B
ONE BYTE DIFFERENT.  D0 BECOMES 10.

  0300  A2 05     LDX #$05
  0302  CA        DEX
  0303  10 FD     BPL $0302
  0305  8E 00 04  STX $0400
  0308  4C 1F FF  JMP $FF1F

  BNE  BRANCH IF RESULT NOT ZERO.
  BPL  BRANCH IF RESULT IS PLUS,
N NEXT P BACK A ANSWER M BOOK Q EXIT
