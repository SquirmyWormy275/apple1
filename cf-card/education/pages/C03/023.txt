PART F: THE COUNTING TRAP

THE ERROR IS THE RANGE, NOT THE
ARITHMETIC. 2 TO THE EIGHTH IS 256,
AND THAT IS THE COUNT OF POSSIBLE
VALUES. BUT THE VALUES RUN 0 THROUGH
255. IF 256 WERE INCLUDED THERE WOULD
BE 257 OF THEM. OFF-BY-ONE ERRORS OF
EXACTLY THIS SHAPE ARE COVERED AGAIN
IN A03.
