B04 SORTING BY HAND
003/030 LEARN


1. LAY THE CARDS OUT AS 5 3 1 4 2.
2. APPLY THE RULE ON THE WORKSHEET:
COMPARE NEIGHBOURS LEFT TO RIGHT, SWAP
WHEN THE LEFT ONE IS BIGGER, REPEAT
PASSES UNTIL A PASS MAKES NO SWAPS.
3. RECORD THE ORDER, COMPARISONS, AND
SWAPS FOR EACH PASS. THAT IS YOUR
FIRST RESULT.

EXPLAIN WHAT HAPPENED

THE RULE IS THE WHOLE ALGORITHM.
COMPARE TWO NEIGHBOURS, SWAP IF THEY
ARE OUT OF ORDER, KEEP GOING. IT IS
CALLED A BUBBLE SORT, BECAUSE ON EACH
PASS THE LARGEST REMAINING VALUE RISES
TO THE END LIKE A BUBBLE.
