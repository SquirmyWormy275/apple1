SORTING FIVE CARDS

WRITE THESE FIVE NUMBERS ON CARDS
AND LAY THEM OUT IN THIS ORDER:

        5   3   1   4   2

THE ONLY MOVE YOU MAY MAKE:
  LOOK AT TWO CARDS THAT ARE NEXT
  TO EACH OTHER.  IF THE LEFT ONE
  IS BIGGER, SWAP THEM.

  THAT COUNTS AS ONE COMPARISON.
  A SWAP IS RECORDED SEPARATELY.

GO LEFT TO RIGHT.  WHEN YOU REACH
THE END, START AGAIN FROM THE LEFT.
