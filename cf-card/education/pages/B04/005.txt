THE ALGORITHM.

COUNTING THE WORK. WITH FIVE CARDS
EACH PASS MAKES FOUR COMPARISONS, AND
YOU NEED SEVERAL PASSES PLUS ONE FINAL
PASS THAT MAKES NO SWAPS TO KNOW YOU
ARE DONE. THAT LAST PASS FINDS NOTHING
AND IS NOT WASTED: IT IS HOW THE
PROCEDURE KNOWS TO STOP. AN ALGORITHM
NEEDS A STOPPING CONDITION IT CAN
ACTUALLY CHECK.

WHAT HAPPENS AS THE LIST GROWS. TEN
CARDS MEANS NINE COMPARISONS PER PASS
AND UP TO NINE PASSES. A HUNDRED CARDS
MEANS NINETY-NINE PER PASS AND UP TO
NINETY-NINE PASSES. THE WORK DOES NOT
DOUBLE WHEN THE LIST DOUBLES; IT
