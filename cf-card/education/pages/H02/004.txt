H02 ORIGINAL, REPLICA, AND REPRODUCTIO
004/035 LEARN

ORIGINAL MEANS MADE AT THE TIME BY THE
PEOPLE WHO MADE THEM. FOR THE APPLE-1
THAT IS ONE OF ROUGHLY TWO HUNDRED
BOARDS FROM 1976. CALLING SOMETHING AN
ORIGINAL IS A CLAIM ABOUT ITS HISTORY,
AND LIKE ANY CLAIM IT NEEDS EVIDENCE.

REPLICA MEANS MADE LATER TO REPRODUCE
THE DESIGN, AND SOLD AS WHAT IT IS.
THE HONESTY IS BUILT INTO THE WORD. A
REPLICA 1 PLUS IS A REPLICA, ITS
MANUAL SAYS SO, AND NOBODY IS
DECEIVED.

REPRODUCTION USUALLY MEANS A CLOSER
COPY, REPRODUCING APPEARANCE AS WELL
AS FUNCTION. THE DISTINCTION FROM
REPLICA IS SOFT AND PEOPLE USE BOTH
