A REWRITTEN STEP 2:

TAKE ONE SLICE. IDENTIFY ITS TWO LARGE
FLAT FACES. CHOOSE ONE OF THEM. USING
A KNIFE, TRANSFER BUTTER FROM THE
PACKET TO THAT FACE AND MOVE THE KNIFE
ACROSS THE FACE UNTIL THE BUTTER
COVERS IT IN AN EVEN LAYER ROUGHLY ONE
MILLIMETRE THICK, STOPPING AT THE
EDGES. REPEAT FOR THE SECOND SLICE.

THAT IS ABOUT 55 WORDS REPLACING 5. A
TENFOLD EXPANSION IS NORMAL, AND
LEARNERS SHOULD BE TOLD SO, BECAUSE
MOST ASSUME THEY HAVE DONE IT BADLY.
