SORTING.

AMBIGUITY HIDES IN ORDINARY WORDS.
"SOME BREAD" DOES NOT SAY HOW MUCH, OR
THAT IT SHOULD BE TWO SLICES, OR THAT
THEY SHOULD BE SEPARATE. "SPREAD
BUTTER ON IT" DOES NOT SAY ON WHICH
SURFACE, HOW MUCH, OR THAT ONLY ONE
SIDE OF EACH SLICE IS INVOLVED. "ADD
FILLING" DOES NOT SAY WHAT, OR WHERE,
OR THAT THE SECOND SLICE THEN GOES ON
TOP. "CUT IT" DOES NOT SAY WHERE, HOW
MANY TIMES, OR IN WHAT DIRECTION.

A HUMAN READER SUPPLIES ALL OF THAT
FROM EXPERIENCE WITHOUT NOTICING. THAT
SUPPLYING IS EXACTLY WHAT A COMPUTER
CANNOT DO.
