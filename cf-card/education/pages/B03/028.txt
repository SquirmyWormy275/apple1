NO SINGLE ANSWER. COMMON ONES: "USING
A KNIFE" ASSUMES THE FOLLOWER CAN HOLD
AND MOVE A KNIFE; "AN EVEN LAYER"
ASSUMES THEY CAN JUDGE EVENNESS; "THE
PACKET" ASSUMES THEY CAN IDENTIFY IT.

THE GOOD ARGUMENTS FOR RELYING ON IT:
THE FOLLOWER IS A PERSON WITH HANDS
AND ORDINARY EXPERIENCE, AND
SPECIFYING HOW TO HOLD A KNIFE WOULD
NEVER END. THE GOOD ARGUMENT AGAINST:
IF THE FOLLOWER IS GENUINELY A
MACHINE, EVERY ONE OF THOSE IS A REAL
GAP.
