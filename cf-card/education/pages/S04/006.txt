(C) IS A FACT. SOMEBODY DID THE THING
AND RECORDED WHAT HAPPENED. IT HAS A
WHO, A WHEN, AND AN OBSERVED RESULT.
NOTICE HOW MUCH LESS IT CLAIMS THAN
THE OTHER TWO. IT DOES NOT SAY WHY THE
DISPLAY GARBLED OR WHAT IS BROKEN. A
FACT IS OFTEN NARROW, AND ITS
NARROWNESS IS THE POINT.

THE ASYMMETRY WORTH CARRYING AWAY.
FACTS ARE EXPENSIVE AND SPECIFIC.
MODELS ARE CHEAP AND GENERAL. A MODEL
CAN COVER A THOUSAND MACHINES; A FACT
COVERS ONE MACHINE ON ONE DAY. WHEN
SOMEONE OFFERS YOU A MODEL WHERE YOU
ASKED FOR A FACT, THEY ARE USUALLY NOT
LYING. THEY ARE ANSWERING AN EASIER
QUESTION.
