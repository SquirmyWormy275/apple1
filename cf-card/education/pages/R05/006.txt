EXTENDING THE ENCODING COSTS BITS.
WANT TEMPO? THAT IS ANOTHER NUMBER.
WANT LOUDNESS PER SLOT? THAT IS NO
LONGER ONE BIT PER SLOT. WANT NOTE
LENGTHS? YOU NEED A WAY TO SAY "THIS
EVENT CONTINUES INTO THE NEXT SLOT,"
WHICH MEANS A SECOND BIT PER SLOT,
DOUBLING THE SIZE.

EVERY FEATURE COSTS SPACE, AND
CHOOSING WHICH TO KEEP IS THE DESIGN.
THE 8-SLOT, 1-BIT-PER-SLOT ENCODING IS
NOT PRIMITIVE; IT IS A CHOICE THAT
KEEPS EXACTLY ONE PROPERTY AND IS VERY
CHEAP.
