TWO PEOPLE HANDED $88 WILL CLAP THE
SAME *PATTERN* AND IT WILL NOT SOUND
THE SAME. NEITHER OF THEM IS WRONG,
BECAUSE THE BYTE NEVER CLAIMED TO
SPECIFY THOSE THINGS.

THAT GAP IS THE LESSON. AN ENCODING
CAPTURES WHAT IT WAS DESIGNED TO
CAPTURE AND SILENTLY DISCARDS
EVERYTHING ELSE. THE DISCARDING IS
INVISIBLE: $88 LOOKS COMPLETE. NOTHING
ABOUT IT ANNOUNCES THAT TEMPO IS
MISSING.
