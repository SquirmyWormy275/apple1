COMPARE / INTERFACE CONTRACT
----------------------------------------
A PROGRAM DOES NOT NEED TO ASK:

"IS VIDEO BUILT FROM 2504 CHIPS?"

IT NEEDS THE I/O BEHAVIOR IT EXPECTS.

IF IMPORTANT SOFTWARE-VISIBLE
BEHAVIOR IS PRESERVED, HARDWARE
BEHIND THE INTERFACE MAY CHANGE.

THAT IS WHY A REPLICA CAN FEEL
FAMILIAR WITHOUT COPYING EVERY
ORIGINAL SUPPORT CIRCUIT.

COMPATIBLE DOES NOT MEAN IDENTICAL.
